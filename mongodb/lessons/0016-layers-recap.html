Phase 3 recap: one review across the whole cluster

Follow one review from the driver to a shard's disk and its secondaries, naming the layer at each step and the number that proves it. No new lab: every number comes from lessons 6–15.

Your win today: you can take a vague complaint ("reviews are slow", "a review disappeared", "one shard is on fire") and say which layer to look at first, which metric to read, and which lesson explains it.

0 · Warm-up from lessons 3 and 9

  1. Lesson 3: a query reads pages that are not in the WiredTiger cache. Whose time is that, and where does it show up in the slow log?
  2. Lesson 9: how many threads does MongoDB 8.0 use per client connection?

Answers: (1) The command's own work: it's in workingMillis (and storage.data.bytesRead/timeReadingMicros). (2) One dedicated thread per connection.
